TOPIC 1
Working Title:
Why Do You Keep Eating When You're Not Hungry?
Central Mystery:
If your stomach is already full, why does your brain still tell you to eat?
Why Viewers Care:
Almost everyone has eaten after being full—especially snacks, desserts, or food while watching TV.
Curiosity Hook:
"Your stomach is full. You know you're full. So why are you still standing in front of the refrigerator?"
Questions:
- Is hunger actually coming from the stomach?
- Why can we always find room for dessert?
- Why does TV make us eat more?
- Why do boredom and stress trigger eating?
- Can we train ourselves to recognize real hunger?

Visual Opportunities:
Full stomach → refrigerator → snacks → TV → brain → dessert → hunger vs craving comparison.
